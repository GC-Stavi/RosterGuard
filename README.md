## Overview

RosterCheck gives Alliance Airlines pilots and cabin crew a fast, plain-language way to verify their rosters against Enterprise Agreement (EA) provisions — directly in the browser, with no app or account required.

---

## Features

- **EBA compliance checking** — Validates rosters against Enterprise Agreement rules for both pilot and cabin crew
- **Rule deviation flagging** — Identifies potential issues including:
  - Early starts
  - Minimum rest day (RDO) requirements
  - Consecutive duty limits
  - Required rest periods between duties
- **Calendar export** — Exports your roster to ICS format for import into any calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, etc.)
- **No install required** — Runs entirely in the browser; nothing to download or set up

## Background

Built by **Craig Abela**, an E190 Line Captain at Alliance Airlines with 31 years of aviation experience.

Developed independently to give crew an accessible, easy-to-understand tool for checking their EA entitlements — without having to wade through the full document or rely on informal advice.

1. Open [rostercheck.netlify.app](https://rostercheck.netlify.app) in any browser
